What we do

Industrial execution from machine foundation to commissioning.

GEW combines mechanical erection, fabrication, alignment, piping, plant maintenance and workforce deployment for demanding pulp, paper and process-plant environments.

01

Machine Erection & Commissioning

Turnkey Pulp & Paper Machine mechanical installation for board machines, Fourdrinier lines, wet ends, presses, dry ends and finishing systems.

02

Heavy Hydraulic Presses

Erection of high-capacity hydraulic hot/cold presses, loading cages, power packs, compressors and interconnected circuits.

03

Pipeline & Utility Networks

Fabrication, routing, erection and testing of process water, stock, vacuum, steam, condensate, hydraulic and thermic-fluid lines.

04

Precision Alignment

Optical theodolite and master-level alignment of sole plates, rolls, shafts, gearboxes, motors and rotating equipment.

05

Structures & Fabrication

Heavy machine frames, platforms, walkways, catwalks, staircases, crane rails, supports and structural retrofits.

06

Manpower & Site Crews

Large-scale deployment of riggers, fitters, welders, helpers, supervisors and skilled labour for fabrication, erection, pipeline, electrical work and maintenance.

07

Electrical Works & Electrical Manpower

Electrical installation and industrial electrical support, with electricians, helpers and technicians for plant projects, erection, maintenance and shutdown activities.

08

Instrumentation Works & Manpower

Instrument installation, field-device mounting, tubing, impulse-line support, cabling support, calibration assistance and skilled instrumentation technicians and helpers.
